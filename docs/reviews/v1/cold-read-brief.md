# The cold read of v1: the reader's brief

This is the whole of what the cold reader was given, apart from its directory's path.

---

You are a reader with nothing but a zip. The zip is the bootstrap kit for a ten-day hackathon. In your directory there
is one file, `softfloat-bootstrap-kit-v1.zip`. Unpack it there, and answer the questions below from what it holds.

The rules of this reading:
- **Read only inside your directory.** Do not open, list or search any file outside it, and do not use the web. If
  you find you need something outside the kit, say so in your answer.
- **You may run commands inside your directory,** such as unpacking the zip or a command the kit tells you to run.
- **Answer from the kit alone.** For each answer, name the kit file, and the section where you can, that it comes
  from. Where the kit does not say, answer "not found in the kit" rather than guess. Where you infer, mark the answer
  BELIEVED.
- **Read as much of the kit as you need,** in the order it tells you to.

The questions:

1. You are the lead, and you have just unpacked the kit. What do you do first, what must you see, and what do you do
   if you do not see it?
2. Which of Logan's repositories is off limits? What counts as that repository? What do you do if you opened it by
   accident?
3. What sends a parcel's work back to its author? What happens to a sentence in the work that claims more than is
   true, when nothing else sends the work back?
4. Describe the ledger: where it lives, who writes which file, how an entry is corrected, and how a heading's time
   is written.
5. When must a parcel read the ledger?
6. In the middle of its work, a parcel has a question that only the lead can answer. What does it do?
7. Who holds the keys of the planted faults, and when are they revealed? What does a verifier do with a finding it
   suspects is a plant?
8. Who controls the Pico, and how does a parcel get time on it?
9. Is the IEEE 754 standard in the kit? Where does it come from, and what is its role in the work?
10. May a parcel commit and push? May the lead?
11. Should a parcel run the project's full test suite?
12. Name the four evidence tags, and say what each one means.
13. On day 1, what does the lead settle with Logan before the first dispatch?
14. Where do the filled copies of HARNESS.md and BOARD.md go, and why not in the kit?
15. What verdicts can a verifier give, and what does READY mean?

Then: anything the kit left unclear, contradicted itself on, or did not tell you that a lead would need on day 1.

Your final message is your answer: number each one to match its question, and keep each to a few sentences with its
source. Say also what you ran, and how long the kit took to read.
